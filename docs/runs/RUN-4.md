### Where the pages disagreed, and the ruling

1. **Scale.** The room ten times over, round the 10 cm engine body: his 1 cm, chosen on 13 October (`docs/runs/RUN-2-TEN-TIMES.md:5`). His garage's land is ten times already; `LEAD.md:24-26` reads otherwise. He played it tonight and still asked, so no number is the answer: the land must look and feel that size.
2. **Biomes.** One list, WORLD-19's: steppe, savannah, grass plain, lake country, moor. WORLD-13 adds local grounds: road, dust, wet toe, scree, rim sand. Other pages add none. Colour shapes the land and the land places the water: a blue floor is dented deeper, and its lakes still stand only in the hollows. The roles LOOK-6 waits for (sand, dry grass, dust silt) land in CR-3.
3. **Niches.** Decided only in `pipeline/landscape/generator/habitat.py` (FLORA-3), from WORLD-12's and WORLD-13's maps. LOOK-5's maps only draw.
4. **Kit.** One: `village_kit.py` (VILLAGE-7, 19). WORLD writes points (cairns, the midwinter stone); the kit builds them. Of 512 objects (`pipeline/landscape/export/exporter.py:83`): villages 160, world 96, flora 32 (provisional).
5. **Budget.** One: LOOK's. 16.7 ms at p95, 12.5 ms of GPU in ten lines, gated by LOOK-1's `-Budget`. It is not paid yet. Today's content costs 14.0 to 15.5 ms of GPU (`docs/look/reviews/run3/after-perf/timings.json`) and its lines allow 10.3. The one cut measured so far saved about 1 ms and was refused as visible (`docs/look/reviews/run3/README.md:61`). So LOOK-2, 22 and 23 are the run's first GPU work, all in wave 1, and no wonder that adds cost comes before them. From LOOK-23 on, such a packet merges only while the ledger shows its line has room. The hours' line (0.4 ms) is the most at any one hour, never a sum: dawn's mist, the day's beam and the night's lights take turns, and `-Budget` runs at dawn, noon, rain and lamps-on. A new line caps draws at 1,500 a camera (1,257 at the spawn today, with 10 sets; the run plans 54).
6. **Doubles.** The Dive: LOOK-13, then MENU-11, then LOOK-20, on the same files. The call: BODY-11 owns the key and delays, LOOK-12 every sound.
7. **Ways up.** WORLD-4's benches are the gate; FLORA-5 hangs a vine only where it opens nothing early; BODY-5 re-proves both. One file, `reach.py`, in that order.
8. **A course** is one band's climb. The gates were sized for a metre of climb a metre of bar, but the bar climbs 1.5 (`game/rules/storybook_wild/v5.json:102`): on land made before BODY-21 a band-2 bar tops band 3's gate, 1.2 m against 1.18 m. BODY-21 mended the gates in wave 1, before any bed or bench is set on them (built, and integrated on 24 November): `gates.cap` carries the pack's number (`pipeline/landscape/generator/gates.py:28`; the arithmetic at `:51-54`), a course is 0.6 m, and every gate stands half as high again: 0.58, 1.18, 1.78 and on to 5.98 m, the knack shortcuts 0.66 and 1.32 m. A lift place's rim follows (`fractals.LIFT_RIM_M`, 6.5 m, over the 6.0 m the top band climbs). Provisional; he feels it in play, and what to feel for is the first ledge: on regenerated land it is 0.58 m and takes 97 percent of the 0.4 m starting bar, where the old 0.38 m took 63 percent, so a first climb empties the bar again. Land made before keeps its old gates until it is regenerated (ruling 10). The summit is WORLD-4's to re-site: on the stand-in it no longer waits for a return visit (the WORLD page's row). BODY-5 re-proves after v6.
9. **The rim.** One table, in section 4, holds lip, tile edge, sand seam, wrap and openings. WORLD-2 writes it, BODY-4 and LOOK-24 read it, WORLD-5 exports it as `x_landscape_rim`. Plants stop at the tile edge (FLORA's 10 m past the lip goes). One dune function under `Seams/` serves LOOK-8 and BODY-13.
10. **Regenerations.** The stand-in basin at each wave's end; his garage in wave 5 and again after wave 6, each on his yes (D3).
11. **Rules v6,** written by six packets, is a draft behind `--rules-version=6` until BODY-7 pins it. Wave 6 writes v7 (R4-9).
12. **The map.** Chalk outline always; pencil for what a found cairn sees; ink for ground walked; all from today's discovered block.
13. **Cut steps.** WORLD-4 proves them, VILLAGE-6 dresses them, BODY-9 prices them.

15. **One wind.** WORLD-13, FLORA-13, LOOK-8 and VILLAGE-16 lean on a home wind, "the only compass". 18. **Rain and the skin.** Rain falls 150 s in every 360 (`NatureState.cs:39-44`). The worldbook bars a fall the child did not choose, so rain never drops a walker: it shuts still water to a new crossing, and the skin holds under a walker already out until they reach ground (BODY-17).
19. **Likeness.** Nothing is copied from another game, film or book. Builders flagged the seed glide as like a shipped backyard-scale game; a leaf held overhead in rain and dust creatures in house corners have well-known kin in film. Before a packet marked **likeness** is dispatched, a reviewer who built none of it searches for the nearest shipped work, writes five lines (what is alike, what is ours, what changes), and the integrator rules. No download. Each page names its fallback from nature.
