**How a lane reads his words** (his charter, whole at `LEAD.md:88`): "extrapolate off the intent and spirit of my instructions ... be expansive, be creative, do research, and go beyond what I initially propose, but keep the themes intact."

- His examples are themes and a floor: not a checklist, not a ceiling.
- The themes: a real room made vast and wild for a body one centimetre tall; every part plainly the room and plainly living land; honest physical logic; a lived-in world; storybook, not photographic; a game a child can love.
- Research the real world, then invent. Build in what goes beyond his words, marked **beyond what was asked**, with an off switch where it draws. He vets it in play: never make an idea a question for him.
- Nothing else loosens: AGENTS.md; nothing copied from another game, film or book; the family writes every word a player reads; no download, purchase or new dependency; his real garage in counts and kinds only.
