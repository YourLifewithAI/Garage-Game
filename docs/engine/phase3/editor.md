# Manual invention editor v1

> **Kernel record, generalized for rooms in Run 1 (P1).** The kernel was built for the retired Barton workshop and is kept. Run 1 replaced its plot rectangle with the room's bounds, its protected garden with locks, its terrain sampler with a physics surface query, its fixture identities with `player:local` and `companion:local`, and its 1.7 m GDScript body with the 10 cm C# body (see [command-host.md](command-host.md), [body-and-physics.md](body-and-physics.md) and the [room-scale direction](../../ROOM-SCALE-DIRECTION.md)). Integration-scene and checkpoint references below may point at files now on the `geography-era-final` branch.

> **Retired for players (6 October, second playtest).** The playable room no longer shows this workshop: the command host sets the runtime's `workshop_enabled` to false, so there is no INVENTIONS panel, no editor and no B, F, E, V, Q or K key (Q and E turn the isometric view instead). The editor, its validation, budgets, receipts and undo stay in the kernel and its suites, which build the runtime with the workshop on; the Run 2 building kit may reuse them.

The in-game workshop edits the portable `garage_game.creation` source rather than a placed mesh. A player starts from a blank design or one of five data templates, then edits named parts, approved materials, typed behavior parameters and directed wires. Part deletion removes behaviors attached to that part and their wires; behavior deletion removes its wires. Draft undo and redo keep up to 32 local states. They do not revert shared world history.

The three-column panel keeps the catalog, private 3D study, and details visible at 1280 × 720. The main body scrolls at smaller sizes so Test and Confirm stay pinned; a 900 × 600 layout check covers the two action buttons. The private study supports mouse-drag orbit and clamped wheel zoom, and draws a copper wire guide around the selected part without changing its shared material. Selecting a behavior follows the part it controls. Shift-drag or switch on **Move part** to reposition the selected part horizontally in the private study. Movement snaps to 5 cm, stays within the source's ±4 m part limits, updates the part and guide immediately, and records a whole drag as one draft undo step. The design uses deep pine, blue-green, sage and copper tones to belong to the grounded painterly workshop while retaining readable controls. Controls have stable names for automated interaction.

**Private Test** recompiles source through `creation_compiler.gd`, checks the world and permission revisions captured when the editor opened, and asks the host for a read-only placement preflight. Invalid placement, locked (protected) space, the room's bounds, an uneven or missing support surface, occupancy, permissions, or capacity receive a message before Confirm is enabled; the draft stays intact. On success, Test previews only nodes reachable through the shared `creation_execution.gd` graph. Timer and proximity triggers are simulated once. A local dummy shows bounded wind and glide cues, while lights and rotors run only for their declared durations. The preview has its own `World3D`, never publishes to authority, and frees its scene and stops rendering when closed. It is an explanatory cue, not a full physics or multiplayer simulation; the placed runtime remains the functional acceptance surface.

**Confirm** sends the still-tested source, placement and cached revisions to the host-owned runtime command service; in the game the runtime forwards it as a `garage_game.command` through the command host. Placement controls span the room's floor area in 5 cm steps, and the support height is probed just above the height the draft was opened at. Any compiler, authority, stale-revision or occupancy error leaves the draft intact. Closing an unfinished new design keeps its source, placement and draft history for this local session; reopening still requires a fresh Test against current revisions, while choosing Blank design explicitly starts over. A placed invention can be reopened for revision or removed through an explicit confirmation. The Access pane controls the player's consent to effects, the companion's consent and whether the companion may build; an explicit world review refreshes cached revisions without changing the draft. If that placed invention changed, the editor refuses to rebase its source silently.

JSON import rejects files over 32 KiB before parsing, then compiles the source before replacing the draft. Export writes normalized portable source only after it compiles. No editor field can grant identity, ownership, permissions, arbitrary scripts or shaders.

The focused `invention_editor_smoke.gd` checks template editing, undo/redo, direct Shift-drag source editing and one-step gesture undo, typed wind normalization, oversize-import preservation, valid export, read-only protected-placement rejection, reachable-only preview execution, effect expiry, stale permission rejection, explicit refresh, separate preview world, selection guides, orbit and zoom bounds, suspended drafts, cleanup and 900 × 600 action visibility. The integrated map flow (`manual_invention_integration.gd`) is on the `geography-era-final` branch; the room equivalent is the command-host test. Neither check is a target-device performance benchmark or evidence of remote multiplayer.
